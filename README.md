# Stanley Ngugi

Independent AI researcher working on reinforcement-learning environments, formal methods, and executable verification.

## Current work

### [native-verify](https://github.com/stanleyngugi/native-verify)

An execution-as-verification environment for language-model reinforcement learning. Models produce restricted computational artifacts; the environment sanitizes them, inserts hidden cases, compiles them into Lean, and derives reward from execution.

### [Grammars for AI Proof Steps](https://github.com/stanleyngugi/ai-proof-grammars)

Experiments and technical writing on grammars for AI-generated mathematical proof steps, including constrained decoding, saved model outputs, provenance, and reproducible analysis.

## Earlier research

- [Surgical Knowledge Rewrite in Compact LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07075) — an early exploratory study of circuit-localized, two-stage IA³ knowledge editing.
- [Taming Incidental Polysemanticity in Toy Models](https://stanleyngugi.netlify.app/posts/taming_polysemanticity) — a research note on training choices and feature-entanglement proxies in toy networks.

These projects are preserved as part of my research history; my present focus is RL environments and formal verification.
